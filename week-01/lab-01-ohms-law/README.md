# Lab 01 — Ohm's Law Verification

## Objective
Verify Ohm's law experimentally using a low-voltage DC source and a known resistor.

## Why It Matters
Ohm's law is foundational to circuit analysis, component selection, electrical troubleshooting, and the design of mechatronic systems. Understanding the relationship between voltage, current, and resistance allows engineers to predict circuit behavior before applying power.

## How It Works
An Arduino Uno provides a nominal 5 V DC supply to a resistor connected between the 5 V and GND terminals. We calculate the expected current using Ohm's law and compare our predictions with measurements obtained using a digital multimeter.

## Equipment
- Arduino Uno and USB power cable
- Breadboard and jumper wires
- 330 Ω resistor
- Commercial Electric DMM-8301S
- Alligator clip adapters
